We get some version of the same question before most orders: how long will this take, and what happens if it isn't right when it arrives. Fair questions for a bag you're meant to carry for years — you should know what you're getting into before you check out, not after. Here's what we can tell you plainly.

## How long does an order take to process?

Every bag ships from our workshop, not a warehouse. Once an order comes in, we check it against stock, pack it by hand, and get it out the door. We don't hold orders waiting to batch them — the goal is to get your bag moving as soon as it's ready, not on some fixed weekly schedule.

## Where does North Leather ship?

We ship to customers browsing from [our shop](https://0b12a1e0.run.linkworld.ai/shop). If you're unsure whether we reach your region, place the order — checkout will only confirm a shipment we can actually fulfill, so you won't be charged for a delivery we can't make.

## How will I know my order shipped?

You'll get a confirmation once your order is placed and another when it's on its way. If you don't see either, check your spam folder first — after that, reach out and we'll sort it directly. We're a small workshop, not a call center, so you'll hear from someone who actually knows where your bag is.

## What if my bag arrives and isn't right?

Tell us. A stitch that isn't sitting right, a mismatch between what you ordered and what showed up — that's on us to fix, not on you to live with. We'd rather make it right than have a bag out in the world that doesn't meet the standard we hold it to.

## Does the leather need special care after it arrives?

Not much, and that's by design. Full-grain leather doesn't want fussing over — it wants to be used. Keep it out of standing water and away from direct heat (radiators, car dashboards in summer), and wipe off dust or grit with a dry cloth now and then. Beyond that, the leather takes care of itself: it darkens, softens, and picks up character from actual use, which is the point. If you want the long version of how that aging process looks over years rather than weeks, we've documented it — see [Made to last, not to impress](https://0b12a1e0.run.linkworld.ai/blog/2026-07-27-made-to-last).

## What if I need to return or exchange a bag?

If a bag doesn't work out, get in touch and we'll walk you through it. Because every piece is cut and stitched from a real hide with its own grain and marks, we handle returns individually rather than through an automated form — a person will look at your specific case and tell you exactly what to do next.

## Why does all this matter more for a bag like this?

Because we're not asking you to buy something disposable. A satchel or tote from us is meant to be the last one you need for a long while, which means the ordering experience should feel as considered as the stitching. If something about your order feels off at any point — before it ships, while it's in transit, or after it arrives — we'd rather hear about it than have you quietly disappointed.
